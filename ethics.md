# Ethics and Authorization Notice

MasterBlaster P0 is for explicitly authorized assessment planning, simulation, and reporting workflows only.

P0 does not run live tools or access targets. Even simulator jobs must be bound to a tenant, client, engagement, adapter manifest, target, expiration time, and policy decision. The runner validates these constraints independently of the UI.

Do not use future versions of this project against systems unless you have explicit written authorization and a reviewed rules-of-engagement record. Do not place secrets in logs, prompts, fixtures, job payloads, or source control.

Reports generated by the simulator are drafts. They are not compliance attestations, certifications, or proof that a target is secure.
